How it works

Four steps, and your team stays in charge of every one that matters.

  1. Export your claims

    Use the export from your practice software. Every system lays its columns out differently, so we match yours with a short mapping file.

  2. Remove who the patient is

    Names, ID numbers and medical aid numbers are never copied. Patient and doctor IDs become codes before the file leaves your computer.

  3. Claims are checked

    Each claim gets a risk score. When something looks wrong, it gets a flag with a reason you can act on.

  4. Your team decides

    Reviewers mark each flag as a confirmed error, a false alarm or corrected. Every decision is logged with who made it and when.

What it checks

Clear rules that explain themselves, plus a statistical second opinion for what rules cannot see.

Missing informationRequired fields left blank, or supporting information not attached.
DuplicatesThe same claim submitted twice, or resubmitted a day apart.
Date problemsA claim submitted before the service took place.
Unusual feesAn amount far from what your practice normally bills for that service. After-hours premiums are allowed for.
Coding mismatchesA service that does not normally go with the diagnosis, unless a second diagnosis explains it.
UpcodingA follow-up visit billed as a standard consultation.
Too many visitsA fourth visit by the same patient within ten days.
Second opinionA model flags claims that resemble ones that previously needed correction. It gives a priority, not a verdict.

You stay in control

The tool recommends. It never approves, rejects or changes a claim.

  • Personal details stay with you. Identifiers are replaced with codes using a key that stays on your computer. Without it, nobody can link the codes back to patients.
  • POPIA applies. Health information is special personal information. We would sign a written agreement first, and we ask your information officer to review it before any data moves.
  • Pilot data has one use. We propose using it only for the pilot, storing it securely, and deleting or returning it when the pilot ends.

Questions

Does it submit or reject claims?

No. It marks claims for review and explains why. Your team decides what happens to each one, and every decision is recorded.

Which practice software does it work with?

Any system that can export claims to a spreadsheet or CSV file. We have tested the importer on artificial exports in two different layouts, and we match each practice’s columns with a small mapping file. We have not yet tested it on a real practice system.

How accurate is it?

We do not know yet on real claims. Tests on artificial data show the checks work as designed, but those results say little about real life. Measuring this is the purpose of the pilot.

Is patient information safe?

Identifying details are removed on your computer before anything is shared, and the key to the codes stays with you. A written agreement and a review by your information officer come before any data moves. This page is not legal advice.

Pilot terms are agreed with each practice. Get in touch and we will talk it through.
